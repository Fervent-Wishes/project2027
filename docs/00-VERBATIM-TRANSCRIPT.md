## 5. Legal structure

One important distinction: the Heritage Foundation is a 501(c)(3) research and educational organization, while Heritage Action for America is a separate 501(c)(4) advocacy organization. <Cite ref="turn278786search7"/>

Fervent Wishes Co. can remain the creative and strategy company. We should investigate forming separate nonprofit entities for charitable research and lobbying, with independent financial accounting and appropriate legal oversight. A 501(c)(3) cannot support or oppose political candidates; a 501(c)(4) has more freedom to lobby and limited electoral activity. <Cite refs={["turn625893search0","turn625893search11"]}/>
